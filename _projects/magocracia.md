Magocracia is an action rpg where you take the mantle of a retired detective in a magical world modeled after the 1920s, returning to a city now filled with gangs, crime and corruption. After the misterious death of a dear friend, it's obvious that someone must make things right again.

You'll take on this journey with a growing arsenal of magical abilities that can be developed and improved, obtaining information as you move closer to the real culprit. Will you be able to find out and defeat whoever is behind it all?
