Magocracia is an action RPG where you take the mantle of a retired detective in a magical world modeled after the 1920s, returning to a city now filled with gangs, crime and corruption. After the mysterious death of a dear friend, it's obvious that someone must make things right again.

You take on this journey with a growing arsenal of magical abilities that can be developed and improved, gathering information as you move closer to the real culprit. Spells are earned as the plot progresses and upgraded with the magical essence of defeated enemies.
