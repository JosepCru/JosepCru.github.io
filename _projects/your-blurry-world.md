Your Blurry World is a serious game about mental health. It narrates the phases of the depression suffered by its protagonist through an adventure across linear levels that tell her story in a distorted reality.

You walk, jump and push your way through each environment, finding notes and watching the protagonist's memories to piece together how she fell into depression and how she faces it. The game is based on the five stages of depression, with each one shown through a level and its narrative.
