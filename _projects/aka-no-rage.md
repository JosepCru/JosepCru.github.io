Aka No Rage is a side-scrolling 2D rogue-lite made in Unity that mixes traditional Japanese aesthetics with futuristic Neo-Tokyo vibes in a story full of action, agile movement and combat against terrifying yokai.

Akane, a priestess of the last remaining temple, fights through the city with her katana and talisman magic, exorcising yokai and facing bosses to find what caused the invasion.
